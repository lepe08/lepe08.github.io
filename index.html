Esri internship · 2026 · StoryMap + Dashboard

Following the Water: San Gabriel Watershed

How elevation data becomes a conservation decision. The study traces the land that drains to critical habitat for the Santa Ana sucker, a threatened native fish, and shows where the 2020 Bobcat Fire burn scar sends sediment into its streams.

  • Mosaicked and projected USGS 3DEP elevation tiles, then ran Fill, Flow Direction, Flow Accumulation, Snap Pour Point and Watershed.
  • Built a stream network with Con and Stream to Feature and buffered USFWS critical habitat by 250 meters.
  • Published hosted feature services and told the story in a StoryMap and Dashboard with a live USGS stream gauge feed from Living Atlas.

Wildfire Vulnerability Explorer

A tool for Southern California: drop a location and see who is at risk during a fire, by census tract, including who may not be able to evacuate on their own or receive an alert.

  • Built canopy height models from LiDAR to describe vegetation fuel.

  • Ran Getis-Ord Gi* hot spot analysis and published the results in Experience Builder.
