LA County Crash Risk Explorer

Where are LA County's most dangerous roads, and why? Our team mapped about 52,000 crashes from 2025 by their top three causes. I led the spatial analysis: finding where each cause clusters and setting that against poverty and hospital access.

  • Sorted 2025 SWITRS crash records into speeding, unsafe turning and unsafe lane-change crashes, then calculated crash rates and cause shares across 4,652 grid cells.
  • Ran Getis-Ord Gi* hot spot analysis overall and for each cause, finding 351 statistically significant hot spots.
  • Prepared the analysis layers for the team's Experience Builder app, which adds Near Me, a swipe comparison, search and a data table.

Honeybees and Urban Development in the San Gabriel Valley

Does a decade of development push pollinators out? Using 6,370 research-grade iNaturalist honeybee observations and ten years of NLCD impervious surface data, I found that every 99%-confidence hot spot sits on a botanical garden or natural area, and that new hot spots appeared only where land stayed undeveloped.

  • Ran Optimized Hot Spot Analysis (Getis-Ord Gi*) for 2014–2018, 2019–2024 and the full period to see where bee activity clusters and how it shifted.
  • Clipped eleven annual NLCD impervious surface rasters and used Raster Calculator to map 2014–2024 development change.
  • Buffered significant hot spots by 3 miles, the typical honeybee foraging range, to estimate where source colonies are and where the valley has none.

Following the Water: San Gabriel Watershed

How elevation data becomes a conservation decision. The study traces the land that drains to critical habitat for the Santa Ana sucker, a threatened native fish, and shows where the 2020 Bobcat Fire burn scar sends sediment into its streams.

  • Mosaicked and projected USGS 3DEP elevation tiles, then ran Fill, Flow Direction, Flow Accumulation, Snap Pour Point and Watershed.
  • Built a stream network with Con and Stream to Feature and buffered USFWS critical habitat by 250 meters.
  • Published hosted feature services and told the story in a StoryMap and Dashboard with a live USGS stream gauge feed from Living Atlas.
